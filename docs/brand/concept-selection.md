# Public Web Concept Selection

## Selected direction

The accepted concept set lives in `docs/brand/concepts/`. It coordinates directly with the current mobile product: warm ivory canvas, forest hierarchy, coral punctuation, fashion-led media, editorial display type, restrained device framing, and varied section rhythm.

The hero concept is the primary composition reference. The remaining section concepts define rhythm and media treatment rather than literal text or fictional product state. Unsupported copy visible in generated references must not be implemented.

## Implementation inventory

- 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content width near 1440px.
- Compact sticky header; standalone mark plus tracked wordmark.
- Hero split approximately 5/7 between copy and a dominant product composition.
- Fraunces display headlines, Manrope body and interface text.
- `#F6F2EA` page canvas, `#163D32` primary, `#F06543` accent.
- One dominant phone per major product story; secondary product imagery stays restrained.
- Section rhythm alternates ivory, paper, sand, and deep forest rather than repeating cards.
- Rounded radii remain related to mobile: 14px controls, 24px surfaces, 30px hero media.
- Shadows remain soft and low-contrast; borders carry most separation.
- Motion is limited to entrance and small hover transitions and is removed for reduced-motion users.

## Fidelity ledger

Implementation QA must compare at least these points:

1. hero headline scale, left/right balance, and first-viewport product visibility;
2. exact forest/ivory/coral palette and restrained accent usage;
3. editorial serif limited to marketing display moments;
4. device frame scale and clean cropping of emulator chrome;
5. asymmetrical product-discovery flow rather than a bento grid;
6. full-bleed forest AI Stylist contrast and prompt-to-outfit clarity;
7. seller section’s clothing-led composition and four-step path;
8. calm, human trust treatment without security theater;
9. compact beta form and substantial closing panel;
10. mobile collapse that preserves a strong first viewport and readable product proof.

## Final implementation QA

The deployed preview was reviewed directly against the accepted concepts on 2026-08-25. It passes the visual signoff for a public beta-recruitment preview: the result reads as a fashion brand, not a generic software template, and remains visibly connected to the mobile product.

Fidelity findings:

1. The desktop hero preserves the concept's editorial scale, split composition, dominant phone, and visible product proof in the first viewport.
2. Forest, ivory, paper, sand, and coral retain their specified roles; coral remains punctuation rather than a competing background system.
3. Fraunces is limited to display moments while Manrope carries navigation, product detail, forms, and longer copy.
4. Product screens use consistent mask-safe phone frames with no emulator chrome, staging URLs, credentials, or debug overlays.
5. Discovery uses asymmetrical editorial bands and product-led image runs instead of a repeated bento or feature-card grid.
6. The Stylist section keeps the full-bleed forest contrast and shows prompt → eligible products → exact PKR total clearly.
7. The seller story remains clothing-led, retains the four-step path, and gives the application CTA its own substantial conversion flow.
8. Trust proof stays calm and specific—profiles, ratings, protected messaging, reporting, blocking, and moderation—with no safety guarantee.
9. The beta form and footer preserve the concept's strong closing panel without fake store badges, launch claims, testimonials, or metrics.
10. Tablet and mobile collapse retain the hero hierarchy, usable navigation, readable product proof, and zero horizontal overflow.

Accuracy-driven copy differences from the generated references:

- The hero implementation removes concept-only launch language and uses the approved promise, “Style finds you here.”
- The Stylist proof uses the verified staged outfit—navy hoodie, light-wash jeans, and black sneakers, totaling PKR 6,700—rather than the concept's illustrative PKR 7,400 set.
- Trust copy removes absolute or implied guarantees and states the actual reputation, communication, reporting, blocking, and moderation boundaries.
- Waitlist consent says exactly how submitted details are used; unsupported unsubscribe automation in the concept is not claimed.
- Generated seller personas are presented only as synthetic product proof, never as testimonials or live-marketplace metrics.

Final evidence is stored in `docs/brand/qa/`: desktop first viewport, desktop full page, tablet first viewport, clean mobile first viewport, and mobile keyboard-focus state.
